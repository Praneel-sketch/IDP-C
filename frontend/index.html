    What this can and cannot tell you
    • Observations come from an automated satellite land-cover product; individual pixels can be misclassified.
    • Timing is bounded by annual observations: “changed by 2021” means between the 2020 and 2021 snapshots.
    • CityChange reports what changed, never why — it makes no causal claims.
